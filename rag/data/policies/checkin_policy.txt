STAYMATE AI - CHECK-IN AND CHECK-OUT POLICY

1. CHECK-IN INFORMATION

The check-in and check-out times for a hotel may vary depending on the hotel.

Users should review the hotel information provided in the applicable hotel details before completing a booking.

2. CHECK-IN DATE

The check-in date is the date on which the guest is expected to arrive at the hotel.

The selected check-in date is part of the booking information.

3. CHECK-OUT DATE

The check-out date is the date on which the guest is expected to leave the hotel.

The selected check-out date is part of the booking information.

4. EARLY CHECK-IN

Early check-in may depend on the hotel's policy and availability.

StayMate AI should not guarantee early check-in unless the applicable hotel information explicitly confirms it.

5. LATE CHECK-OUT

Late check-out may depend on the hotel's policy and availability.

StayMate AI should not guarantee late check-out unless the applicable hotel information explicitly confirms it.

6. HOTEL-SPECIFIC POLICIES

Check-in requirements, identification requirements, early check-in, and late check-out conditions can vary between hotels.

Users should verify hotel-specific requirements before arrival.

7. KNOWLEDGE ASSISTANT LIMITATION

The StayMate AI knowledge assistant should answer check-in and check-out questions only using information available in its knowledge base.

If hotel-specific information is unavailable, the assistant should clearly state that it does not have sufficient information.
