STAYMATE AI - PAYMENT POLICY

1. PAYMENT PROCESS

StayMate AI currently provides a demo payment flow as part of the project.

The demo payment flow is intended to demonstrate the booking and billing workflow.

2. PRODUCTION PAYMENT

The current project does not represent a production payment gateway.

A production deployment would require integration with a real payment service provider.

3. PAYMENT METHOD

The booking flow collects the payment method selected by the user.

The selected payment method is associated with the booking information.

4. BILLING

The billing process calculates the booking amount based on the booking details available to the application.

The billing summary may include:

- Price per night
- Number of nights
- Number of rooms
- Subtotal
- Applicable tax
- Total amount

5. PAYMENT STATUS

The booking record contains payment-related information, including the payment status.

6. PAYMENT QUESTIONS

The StayMate AI knowledge assistant can explain the payment workflow described in its knowledge base.

It should not claim that a real payment has been processed when the application is using its demo payment flow.

7. PRODUCTION LIMITATION

The current demo should not be presented as a real financial transaction system.

A production implementation would require secure payment processing through an appropriate payment provider.
