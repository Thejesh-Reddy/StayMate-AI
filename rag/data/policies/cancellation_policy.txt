STAYMATE AI - CANCELLATION POLICY

1. CANCELLATION INFORMATION

StayMate AI allows authenticated users to manage their bookings through the My Bookings section.

Cancellation availability and refund conditions may depend on the cancellation rules associated with the booking.

2. CANCELLATION OF A BOOKING

Users should review the cancellation conditions associated with their booking before cancelling.

The cancellation process should be performed through the available booking management functionality.

3. REFUND INFORMATION

Any refund depends on the applicable cancellation conditions for the booking.

StayMate AI should not guarantee a refund unless the applicable booking information explicitly states that a refund is available.

4. NON-REFUNDABLE BOOKINGS

Some bookings may have non-refundable conditions.

If a booking is marked as non-refundable, cancellation may not result in a refund.

5. CANCELLATION QUESTIONS

The StayMate AI knowledge assistant can explain cancellation-related information available in its knowledge base.

If the knowledge base does not contain the cancellation conditions for a particular hotel or booking, the assistant should not invent them.

6. CURRENT BOOKING INFORMATION

The knowledge assistant provides information from its stored knowledge base.

For booking-specific information, users should refer to their booking details and the My Bookings section.

7. IMPORTANT LIMITATION

The current StayMate project does not use this document to determine the actual cancellation or refund status of a live hotel booking.

Live booking conditions should be verified from the applicable hotel or booking provider.
