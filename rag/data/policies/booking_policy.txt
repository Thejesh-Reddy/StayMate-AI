STAYMATE AI - BOOKING POLICY

1. HOTEL SEARCH

StayMate AI allows users to search for hotels by destination or location.

Users can search for hotels using a manually entered destination or their current location when location access is available.

Hotel search results are obtained from the configured hotel search service.

2. BOOKING INFORMATION

Before confirming a booking, users must provide the required booking information.

The booking information may include:

- Guest name
- Mobile number
- Email address
- Hotel name
- Hotel address
- Check-in date
- Check-out date
- Number of guests
- Number of children
- Number of rooms
- Room type
- Payment method

3. BOOKING CONFIRMATION

After the booking process is completed, StayMate AI creates a booking record.

The booking contains a unique booking reference.

The booking information is stored in the StayMate database.

Users can view their bookings through the My Bookings section.

4. PAYMENT

StayMate AI currently provides a demo payment flow for the project.

The payment flow does not represent a production payment gateway.

A production version would require integration with a real payment service provider.

5. BOOKING HISTORY

Authenticated users can access their previous bookings through the My Bookings section.

Users can only access bookings associated with their authenticated account.

6. HOTEL PRICES

Hotel prices and availability can change over time.

Current hotel prices should be obtained from the live hotel search results rather than from this policy document.

The StayMate AI knowledge assistant should not provide a current hotel price unless that information is explicitly available in its knowledge base.

7. ACCOUNT REQUIREMENT

Users must authenticate before accessing protected booking functionality.

New users can create an account using email verification and password creation.

8. KNOWLEDGE ASSISTANT

The StayMate AI knowledge assistant answers questions using information available in its knowledge base.

If the requested information is not available in the knowledge base, the assistant should clearly state that it does not have sufficient information rather than inventing an answer.

9. CURRENT INFORMATION

Information such as current hotel prices and live hotel availability should be obtained through the live hotel search system.

The knowledge assistant is intended primarily for answering questions about StayMate, hotel information available in its knowledge base, policies, and other stored knowledge.
